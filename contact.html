How do I register my child?+

Registration is completed online via our secure portal. Select your age group and team preference, then pay the registration fee before the deadline to secure your spot.

What equipment is required?+

Athletes are responsible for bringing a mouthguard, cleats. The league supplies all extra tools, like jerseys and flags for game days.

When are the games scheduled?+

Our schedule is released in early June. Games typically run from late August through early October, with specific dates posted on our league website and social media.

How can I get my child in the league?+

Our league is open to all youth ages 6-18. We encourage parents to reach out to our coaches to discuss their child’s skill level and potential for the team.

Is flag football safe for younger players?+

Yes. Flag football is non-tackle, and our certified coaches teach proper technique, sportsmanship, and safety at every practice and game.
