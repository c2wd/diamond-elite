01 The Club

Not just a team. A diamond in the making.

Diamond Elite is a no-contact, high-energy flag football program where kids learn the game the right way: smart routes, quick hands, and big-hearted teammates. Our coaches are certified, background-checked, and obsessed with development.

Whether it's your child's first snap or fifth season, we build confidence one rep at a time. Pressure creates diamonds. Practice creates champions.

  • USA Football certified coaches
  • Equal playing time
  • Scholarships available
