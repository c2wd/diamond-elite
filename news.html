Lady Cowboys Girls Bring Home the WPA Super Bowl

A season of early practices and late-game grit came together on championship day.

Read the story +

Championship day was everything a season is built for. The Lady Cowboys Girls took the field ready, locked in on the plan their coaches drew up all season and played for each other from the first snap to the final flag pull.

Cleats for Cans: Our Fall Food Drive

Players and families turned game day into a day of giving.

Read the story +

This fall, Diamond Elite teams turned game day into a day of giving. Families dropped canned goods and pantry staples in the donation bins at every home game, and players helped sort and load the donations afterward.

Everything collected went to local neighbors who needed it most. Thank you to every family who gave, and to the volunteers who made the drive run smoothly.

We believe a team is only as strong as the community behind it, and we plan to make the food drive a Spring 2027 tradition too.

8U and 10U Take Home the Brownson House Titles

Two divisions, two championship trophies, one very loud sideline.

Read the story +

Both our 8U and 10U squads walked away from the Brownson House tournament as champions. Across the bracket they showed what Diamond Elite football is about: quick hands, big hearts and teammates who lift each other up.

It was a proud day for the whole club, and a reminder that every rep at practice shows up on game day. Congratulations to every athlete and coach in both divisions.

Free Youth Flag Football Clinic Coming This Spring

New to the game? Come try it, no sign-up fee and no experience needed.

Read the story +

Diamond Elite is planning a free community clinic ahead of the Spring 2027 season, open to any kid curious about flag football. Our coaches will walk through the basics: how to pull a flag, run a route and play as a team.

Every participant goes home with a flag belt to keep. The date and location are still to be announced, so watch this page for details.

Know a family who would love to try it? Pass the word along.
